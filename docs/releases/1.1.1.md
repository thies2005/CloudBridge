# CloudBridge 1.1.1 — Google Play Distribution & 16 KB Page Alignment

- Added the dedicated `play` distribution flavor that disables self-update checks/notifications and omits `REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES` for Google Play policy compliance while keeping the `oss` flavor unchanged for GitHub, Obtainium, and F-Droid.
- Fixed x86_64 native rclone binary alignment to support 16 KB memory page sizes alongside arm64-v8a.
- Updated German and English store listing descriptions, screenshots, and changelogs.
