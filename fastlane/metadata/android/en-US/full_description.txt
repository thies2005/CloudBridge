Manage cloud storage and local files together on Android. Browse folders, move and rename files, and upload or download what you need from your phone or tablet.

CONNECT YOUR STORAGE
Connect rclone-compatible services such as Internxt and Drime. Set up supported providers in the app, or import your existing rclone configuration.

FIND FILES ACROSS DRIVES
Search the current folder, include subfolders, or search across drives with offline indexing enabled. Narrow results by file type or size.

SCHEDULE AND TRACK TRANSFERS
Set up recurring copies and syncs, and see progress and estimated time remaining for active transfers when available.

ENCRYPT, STREAM AND SERVE
Encrypt files before upload with a configured rclone Crypt remote. Stream media or serve files and folders over HTTP, WebDAV, FTP or DLNA.

WORK WITH ANDROID
Share files to CloudBridge from other apps, and use Android’s file picker to access supported SD cards and USB storage. Choose from light, dark and AMOLED themes in the Material 3 Expressive interface.

CloudBridge is open source under the GPLv3 license. It connects to storage you provide. Supported features depend on your provider and configuration.
